Music

In my childhood musical activities I wasn’t sure if I prefer singing or playing, so I did both.

For couple of years I was second voice, first alt in the internationally famous chorus of проф. Венета Тодорова Вичева (Главен диригент на хор „Родни звуци” и хор „Бодра песен” / Изключителен принос в развитието на хоровото дело), honour citizen of the town. It was really hard work, during the weekends including.


My father convinced me to take my violin lessons from a private teacher (instead of the common practice to go to the city musical school). This teacher was Веселин Александров Спиров, Viena educated in music pedagogy and the founder of the local Symphony Orchestra, honour citizen.
