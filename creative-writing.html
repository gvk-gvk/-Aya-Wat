Creative Writing

The beginning of my creative writing was shaped by the rich literary tradition of my birthplace. My talent was recognized and shaped by the best.

As a child, I was fortunate to participate in a children literary group, which would send me every summer to national summer camps in Ravda (Black Sea) for talents, some of them with international participation. It was really unique atmosphere, to meet children at your age, all of them creative a way different of yours. There, I had friends who became caricaturists, painters, musicians, young television stars. I remember one ensemble of Aserbajdgan singers, some of whom became my personal friends.

When I became teenager, a member of national writers association, poet and redactor in the local newspaper (Славчо Николов https://literaturensviat.com/?p=151481) to whom I was sent by the teacher of the kids literary group, helped me become a correspondent of the only local newspaper “The Dawn of Shumen”, which became my first paid job during my high school education. He helped me as well polish my poem for participation in one National Literary Competition, at which I received bronze medal for poetry. I don’t remember receiving anything else but the medal. There was another literary competition, which was really profitable for me at this age - Silistra’s “Golden Wheatear” and the money was exactly on place for my graduation party, pardon ball.
